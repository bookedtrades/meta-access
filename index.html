Meta ads setup

Get your Facebook ads ready to run

Follow these six steps to set up your Meta business portfolio, Facebook Page and ad account, then give our team access so we can build and run your campaigns. Everything stays in your name and under your control.

Time needed

About 20 minutes

Use a computer

Laptop or desktop is much easier than a phone

Have ready

Your personal Facebook login, business details and a payment card

The steps

1

Create your business portfolio

This is the home for your business on Meta. Your Page and ad account will live inside it.

  1. Go to business.facebook.com and log in with your personal Facebook account.
  2. Click Create a business portfolio. If you don't see it, click the business name drop-down in the top left and choose Create a business portfolio.
  3. Enter your business name, your first and last name, and your business email address, then click Create.
  4. Check your inbox and click the link in Meta's email to confirm your business email.
Already have one?If your business already has a portfolio (it may still be called Business Manager), skip this step and use that one.
2

Create your Facebook Page

Creating the Page from inside your portfolio means your business owns it from day one.

  1. In Meta Business Suite, click Settings (the gear icon, bottom left).
  2. Under Accounts, click Pages.
  3. Click the blue Add button, then choose Create a new Facebook Page.
  4. Enter your business name as the Page name and pick a category (for example Concrete Contractor, Landscaper or Builder).
  5. Click Create Page. You can skip the extra Page details for now; we can help fill them in.
3

Create your ad account

This is where your ads run and where your ad spend is charged.

  1. Still in Settings, under Accounts, click Ad accounts.
  2. Click Add, then choose Create a new ad account.
  3. Fill in the details exactly as below, then click Next.
FieldWhat to enter
Ad account nameYour business name + "Ads" (e.g. Smith Concreting Ads)
Time zoneAustralia/Sydney
CurrencyAustralian Dollar (AUD)
  1. When asked who the ad account is for, choose My business.
  2. Agree to the terms and click Create.
  3. If Meta asks you to add people, select yourself, turn on Full control, and click Assign.
ImportantDouble-check the time zone and currency before you click Create. Meta does not let you change either one later without making a new ad account.
4

Add a payment method

Ads can't run until the ad account has a card attached. Meta charges your card directly for ad spend.

  1. In Meta Business Suite, open Billing & payments from the left menu (or go to business.facebook.com/billing_hub).
  2. Use the account selector in the top right to pick the new ad account you made in Step 3.
  3. Go to Payment settings and click Add payment method.
  4. Enter your card details and click Save. Meta may make a small temporary charge to check the card.
CheckMake sure the ad account name in the top right matches the one you just created before you add the card.
5

Add Booked Trades as a partner

This connects our business portfolio to yours. You don't need to share your login or password.

  1. Go back to Settings.
  2. Under Users, click Partners.
  3. Click Add, then choose Give a partner access to your assets.
  4. Paste our Business Portfolio ID below and click Next.

6

Give us full access to your Page and ad account

This screen appears straight after you enter our ID.

  1. Under Pages, select the Page you created in Step 2 and switch on Full control (it may show as "Everything").
  2. Under Ad accounts, select the ad account you created in Step 3 and switch on Full control (it may show as "Manage ad account").
  3. Review the details and click Assign assets.
That's itLet us know once this is done and we'll confirm everything has come through on our side.

Final checklist

Tick these off as you go.

If something doesn't look right

I can't see "Create a new ad account" or "Partners".
You need full control of the business portfolio. If someone else set it up, ask them to give you full control, or ask them to follow these steps.
Meta says the partner ID is invalid.
Use the Copy ID button above instead of typing it, and make sure there are no spaces before or after the number.
My screen looks a bit different.
Meta updates its menus often. The names in this guide should still appear somewhere in Settings, so look for the same words in the left-hand menu.
